LLM Council · Verdict

Chatly — Which features can be locked on lower plans without causing churn?

Five advisors · independent analysis · anonymized peer review · chairman synthesis
The Question
The lecturer recommends locking features on lower plans and unlocking them on higher ones. Which Chatly features can be gated without triggering customer churn? Core value loop = bot auto-replies from an uploaded Knowledge Base (RAG) → detects purchase intent → sends payment invoices.
VERDICT: Gate value-EXPANDING features, never the value-CORE loop. And the real lever isn't feature-gating at all — it's metering messages/channels (which also fixes the "unlimited" margin flaw).

Feature-gating is secondary monetization. The one trap the whole council missed: the message meter is also the core-loop throttle — a silent mid-chat cutoff is the worst churn bomb of all. It must degrade gracefully (soft cap + overage), not hard-stop.

Safe to gate vs. never gate (paid tiers)

Where the Council Agrees

Strong consensus on the gate-ability axis: gate value-expanding features, never the value-core loop. Safe (near-zero churn): API keys+webhooks, AI Insight, multi-business, team seats, channels, analytics depth/export, history retention, KB document count, SLA, and custom-personality on FREE. The Outsider's own words ratify it — "developer toys and multi-shop scale" are fair locks. Equally firm on what stays ungated on every paid tier: bot replies, RAG, payment invoices, AI/HUMAN handoff, Conversion-Rate proof, and ignored-contacts as basic safety. And the unifying lever all five circle: kill "unlimited," gate the message meter — messages are the COGS driver, so metering is simultaneously the primary monetization axis and the margin fix.

Where the Council Clashes

The fight is customer-perceived fairness vs. tidy tiering. The Executor's clean matrix gates KB doc count, personality, and a 7-day FREE retention, and caps FREE at 100 messages. The Outsider (a Rp149K warung owner) and the Contrarian attack exactly these: starving the KB causes thin-KB hallucinations in public WhatsApp threads, damaging the merchant's reputation — a self-inflicted wound, not an upsell. The sharper clash: the Outsider calls human-takeover and ignored-contacts "basic rights," not premium — locking handoff is "a hostage situation." Reviewers noted the Outsider overgeneralizes one persona and offers no pricing structure, but the Contrarian sides with the customer: never gate anything that degrades answer quality or hides ROI. Synthesis: take the Executor's structure, bend it toward the customer on loop-adjacent features.

Blind Spots the Council Caught unanimous in peer review

The message-cap failure mode. Everyone said "gate the meter," but the meter is also the core-loop throttle — the fix and the forbidden gate are the same dial. A hard, silent cutoff mid-conversation kills the bot in a live customer thread: worse churn and reputation damage than any locked feature, because the SMB concludes "it ignores my customers." Nobody designed graceful degradation. Required: soft caps + overage/top-up billing (pay-as-you-go past quota, never hard-stop), owner alerts at 80% and 100%, and auto-throttle-not-kill.

Three more gaps: (1) Grandfathering — killing "unlimited" risks churning the current paying Growth/Pro base; migrate on legacy terms. (2) Downgrade mechanics — what happens to KB doc #26 or business #4 when a plan lapses (read-only freeze, not silent deletion). (3) WhatsApp/Gowa per-conversation pass-through costs + spam/gaming inflating the meter — model true per-message COGS before pricing overage.

The Recommendation do this

Usage metering (messages + channels) is PRIMARY monetization and the margin fix; feature-gating is SECONDARY. Adopt the Executor matrix, corrected per Contrarian/Outsider: kill unlimited → soft caps + overage; gate API+webhooks (Growth+), AI Insight (Pro), multi-business (Growth+), team seats, KB doc count generously, analytics depth/export only, retention window, SLA (Pro). Keep on ALL paid tiers: bot replies, RAG, invoices, AI/HUMAN handoff, Conversion-Rate headline (gate only drill-down/CSV), and a basic ignored-contacts allowance. On FREE: preset-only personality + a doc cap high enough to answer competently (not 3 docs that hallucinate) — the cheapest, lowest-churn push to Starter.

The One Thing to Do First

Ship a planLimits config map + server-side guards in the route handlers for the high-consensus cheap gates (API access, AI Insight, multi-business, team seats, channels, KB doc count, analytics export) — and in the same change, pair the message meter with a soft-cap + overage top-up path (80%/100% alerts, throttle-not-kill), never a silent cutoff. No hard gate ships until the graceful-degradation meter ships alongside it. The guard map makes every other gate a one-line entry; the overage path makes the margin fix safe to turn on.
The five advisors (full responses)
The Contrarian — what backfires downside
Gating the core loop is where this dies. Worst gate: the message cap itself — 100/mo on FREE is a broken bot (one chatty customer burns it, the bot goes silent mid-conversation, the SMB concludes "it ignores my customers" and leaves before seeing value); even Starter's 10K can clip a real seller in a week and read as an outage, not an upsell. Gating Knowledge Base (doc count, personality) is self-sabotage — a thin KB means wrong/hallucinated answers in public WhatsApp threads, damaging the SMB's reputation. Gating Conversion-Rate/Verified Conversion is the dumbest move — that's the ROI proof that justifies the bill; hide it and renewal becomes "Rp349K for what?" The lecturer's error: assuming all features are equally lockable. The core loop is load-bearing. Safe gates only: developer surface (API keys/webhooks/Docs), data export, AI Insight, multi-business, channel count, advanced/real-time analytics granularity, 24/7 + SLA. And kill "unlimited" — it's the margin bomb that funds the churn you're trying to prevent.
The First Principles Thinker — gate the meter, not the feature reframe · co-strongest
Feature-gating exists to convert growth in realized value into growth in revenue without ever charging the customer before they feel the value. A gate works when it sits on an axis that rises naturally as the customer succeeds — hitting the wall feels like graduation, not punishment. It triggers cancellation when it sits on the core value loop, because then it's "pay more to keep what already worked." So the right axis is almost never binary on/off — it's usage/value metrics that scale with the customer's own success: channels, message volume, multi-business, analytics retention depth. Gate the meter, not the feature — and gating message volume also kills the "unlimited" margin flaw simultaneously. Value-core, never gate: the bot reply loop, KB/RAG upload, intent detection + payment invoices, AI/HUMAN handoff, and Conversion-Rate proof (the evidence that justifies the subscription). Value-expanding, safe to gate: API keys + webhooks, AI Insight, deeper analytics (Verified Conversion, Token Usage, longer retention), multi-business, and SLA. Every gate a customer hits should be one they've earned by growing.
The Expansionist — the conversion proof IS the upgrade engine upside
Your strongest pull is Verified Conversion + Conversion-Rate analytics, because they prove ROI in the merchant's own currency: rupiah closed. Gate the depth, not the number. FREE/Starter see the headline conversion rate; Growth+ unlocks buyer lists, transaction drill-downs, attribution, and CSV export. Once a merchant sees "the bot closed Rp40M this month," Rp349K is a rounding error — the product sells its own next tier. AI Insight = the PRO carrot: raw analytics on Growth, AI recommendations ("80% of lost buyers ask about shipping — add it to your KB") on Pro. API + webhooks = a hard Growth/Pro wall with zero churn risk — devs/agencies integrating into checkout/CRM are your highest-LTV, lowest-price-sensitivity segment. Natural expansion lives in three meters: channels (5→15→unlimited), multi-business (gate to Growth+, agencies managing many merchants are pure expansion), and message volume. Tie seats/team handoff to Pro. Don't gate the core loop or KB upload — land everyone on the addictive WhatsApp→bot→invoice loop, let usage and proof pull them up. Gate ambition, never the habit.
The Outsider — warung owner: never lock what lets me sell customer eyes · flagged blind spot
Rp149K is what I can afford. FAIR, I get it: API keys/webhooks/docs locked away — I don't even know what those words mean, that's for people with programmers, of course it costs more. Managing many shops — I have one shop. 15 channels vs 5 — I have one WhatsApp number. Exportable reports, 24/7 support, uptime guarantee for the top plan — nice-to-have for big sellers. INSULTING, I'd quit: if "human takeover" is locked — the whole reason I trust the bot is that I can grab the chat when a customer is angry; lock that and it's a hostage situation. If the bot can't send payment links on my plan — that's literally why I'm paying. If I can't see "verified sales the bot caused" even as a simple number — how do I know it earns its Rp149K? If "ignore these contacts" is locked — my supplier and my mother chat me; the bot shouldn't reply to them; that's basic safety, not a luxury. Lock the developer toys and the multi-shop scale. Never lock the things that let me sell, take over, and prove it made money.
