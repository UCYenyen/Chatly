Chatly — Is the 2-year financial projection credible, and is the pricing right?

Five advisors · independent analysis · anonymized peer review · chairman synthesis
The Question
Evaluate Chatly's 2-Year Financial Projection (Q3 2026 – Q2 2028) and the current website pricing. Live tiers: Starter Rp149K/mo = 10,000 msgs · Growth Rp349K/mo = UNLIMITED msgs (highlighted) · Pro Rp749K/mo = UNLIMITED msgs + 99.9% SLA · yearly 15% off. Projection: 30→1,200 clients in 2 yrs, no churn, 8 founders @ Rp2jt/mo, 1,200 tenants on one Rp400K VPS, break-even Q4 2027, NPV Rp101jt, IRR 25.8%, ROI 159% on Rp500jt pre-seed.
VERDICT: Not credible as-is. Directionally a real business resting on 4–5 load-bearing fictions. The metered Starter tier is fine; the "unlimited" Growth/Pro is the core defect.

Add realistic churn, real salaries, and real infra and the headline returns (NPV Rp101jt, IRR 25.8% — below Indonesian govt bonds) likely go negative. It can still work — but only with value-aligned, capped pricing.

Where the Council Agrees

Four of five converged, ratified by peer review: the projection rests on load-bearing fictions and the "unlimited" tiers are the structural defect. (1) "Unlimited" priced as metered is incoherent — VC is modeled at a capped ~30K-msg count while Growth/Pro promise unlimited; one power user inverts the margin. (2) Zero churn is disqualifying — at 5%/mo the 30→1,200 ramp never completes and Q4 2027 break-even never arrives. (3) The BEP is understated — the table uses Rp16.1jt FC (Growth BEP 55) while the P&L itself ramps personnel to ~Rp108jt/mo (loaded ~Rp124jt → real BEP ~340–400). (4) One Rp400K VPS for 1,200 persistent Gowa sessions is fantasy, and a 99.9% SLA on one box is self-refuting. (5) Rp2jt/mo founder pay is fake burn or a team that leaves.

Where the Council Clashes

The real tension is the Expansionist vs. the other four. The Expansionist argues Chatly is underpriced: it sits on the payment rail, should charge a 0.5–1% GMV take-rate, model NRR >120%, and 2–3× the Rp5.38B ARR via commerce pricing, payments float, and an SMB credit-scoring dataset. The other four argue the model is built on fictions and solvency must be fixed first. The chairman sides with sequencing, not against the insight. The Expansionist is right in the long run — Chatly sells GMV, not messages, and value-aligned pricing is where the margin lives. But you cannot monetize a vehicle that may not run; layering fintech upside onto no-churn, one-VPS, fake-salary foundations is "a solvency problem mistaken for a monetization opportunity." Worse, the take-rate pivot adds BI/OJK licensing liability. Value-pricing is the Series A story, not the pre-seed fix.

Blind Spots the Council Caught surfaced in peer review

(a) Legal/regulatory is an existential binary gate. The unofficial WhatsApp bridge violates Meta ToS; auto-invoicing implicates Bank Indonesia/OJK payment-licensing (PJP/e-money); stored chat/PII vectors implicate UU PDP. The model is "one cease-and-desist or BI ruling from zero" — unquantified by anyone.
(b) FX exposure. Rp500jt is denominated against a USD-priced Gemini API + USD VPS. Uncosted.
(c) The demand/CAC side was never challenged. Every advisor attacked cost, churn, and platform — but all silently accepted that 1,200 paying tenants arrive, with thin marketing, 8 founders, and no funnel/CAC math. The single most unsupported number in the model.
(d) No sensitivity/downside scenario; revenue-recognition and Xendit settlement timing behind the NPV are unaddressed.

The Recommendation honest answer

Is the projection credible? No. It is directionally a real business — genuine market, real product, real conversion analytics — but rests on 4–5 load-bearing fictions: unlimited-but-metered pricing, zero churn, free founders, one VPS, understated BEP. The headline returns are marginal as stated: NPV Rp101jt (~$6,500 after two years of eight people), IRR 25.8% — below Indonesian government bonds for a platform-risk startup. Add realistic churn (3–5%/mo), loaded personnel (~Rp124jt/mo), real infra, and FX, and those returns likely go negative.

Is the pricing right? Partly. The metered Starter (Rp149K / 10K msgs) is fine — honest and value-aligned. The "unlimited" Growth and Pro tiers are the core defect: they make your best customers your least profitable and render unit economics unknowable. The business can work — but only with value-aligned, capped pricing, modeled churn, and loaded costs. Fix the foundation; the GMV upside is the next chapter.

The One Thing to Do First

Replace "unlimited" on Growth and Pro with a fair-use cap + overage (Growth 50K, Pro 200K msgs/mo at ~Rp10/msg over) — and rebuild the model with realistic churn (3–5%/mo) and fully loaded personnel (~Rp124jt/mo) — before showing it to any investor or onboarding at scale. This single change converts the unit economics from fiction to fact and surfaces the true Growth BEP (~400, not 55) on your terms instead of in diligence.
